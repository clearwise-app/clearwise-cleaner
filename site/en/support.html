Common problems

macOS won't open the app

Double-click the app and close the warning. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway" next to Clearwise Cleaner. You only need to do this once.

The app finds very little junk

Full Disk Access is probably off. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access → turn on Clearwise Cleaner and restart the app.

Full Disk Access was lost after an update

macOS may ask again after the app is replaced with a new version. Turn Clearwise Cleaner off and on again in the same list.

No space was freed after cleaning

Files stay in the Trash until you empty it, so mistakes can be undone. Empty the Trash in Finder or with the Empty Trash button in the app.

How to restore a removed file

Open the Trash in Finder, right-click the file and choose "Put Back".
