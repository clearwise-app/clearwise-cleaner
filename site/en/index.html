Features

Nine tools in one window

Pick a section on the left, just like in the app. Screenshots are from Clearwise Cleaner on demo data.

Cleanup Files Checkup

Smart Care

One scan covers junk, leftovers of deleted apps, old files and unused apps. Safe items are selected for you; the rest is your call.

System Junk

App and browser caches (never history, passwords or cookies), caches of Slack, Discord, VS Code and other Electron apps, logs, crash reports, Xcode DerivedData, npm, pip and Homebrew caches, old installers, iPhone firmware and Docker. Three modes: Gentle, Thorough, Maximum.

Uninstaller

Removes an app together with its settings, caches, containers, launch agents and helpers. A separate tab finds what is left of apps you already dragged to the Trash.

Startup Items

What starts with your Mac: agents, system daemons and login items, with owner, signature and state. Turn them off reversibly or move them to the Trash. macOS services are never touched.

Old & Large Files

Files you haven’t opened in months and the biggest files on the disk. Nothing is selected automatically.

Duplicates

Identical files by content, not by name. One copy always stays.

Disk Map

A sunburst chart: click to open a folder, drop what you don’t need into the Collector.

Storage

What fills your disk by category, in percent and gigabytes. Any category opens down to files, even “System Data”.

Mac Health

Battery and SSD wear, free space and Intel-only apps that will lose Rosetta soon. Read-only: it changes and removes nothing.

Honest numbers

“2.1 GB in the Trash”, not “freed”

Until the Trash is emptied the space is still in use, and the app says so. What was deleted for good and what Docker freed are shown on separate lines.

Safety

Careful by default

Everything goes to the Trash

Only the Trash itself is ever emptied for good, and only after a separate confirmation. Deleting permanently right away takes an explicit checkbox.

Protected places

System folders, Keychain, Mail, Messages, iCloud Drive, the Photos and Music libraries and built-in macOS apps are never touched.

Every path is visible

Personal files are never selected automatically, only safe junk. You see the full path of every file before it is removed.

No internet

The app sends and collects nothing: no analytics, no telemetry. This site has no counters or cookies either.

Modes are just checkmarks

Gentle, Thorough and Maximum only tick different items after a scan. The Trash, the latest backup of each iPhone and Docker volumes are never ticked.

Open source

All code is on GitHub under the MIT license. Check exactly what gets removed, or build the app yourself.

For developers

Knows where developer gigabytes pile up

  • Xcode: DerivedData, old archives, Device Support and simulator caches
  • npm, Yarn, pnpm, Bun, pip, uv, Homebrew, Gradle, CocoaPods, SwiftPM and Cargo caches, plus Playwright browsers
  • Docker: build cache, dangling images, stopped containers. Data volumes are never touched
  • iPhone and iPad firmware (.ipsw)

An MCP server for Claude, Cursor and other assistants

The app ships with clearwise-mcp. Your assistant finds junk and removes only what you approve. Anything destructive runs as a dry run first.

Install

Installed in a minute

  1. Download the DMG

    Clearwise-Cleaner-1.2.1.dmg, 9.1 MB. Works on Apple silicon and Intel.

  2. Drag to Applications

    Open the image and drag Clearwise Cleaner into the Applications folder.

  3. Confirm the first launch

    System Settings → Privacy & Security → “Open Anyway”. You only do this once.

  4. Grant Full Disk Access

    Optional, but without it some junk stays hidden. The app shows you where to turn it on.

Why macOS asks for confirmation

The app is not notarized by Apple yet; that requires a paid Apple Developer account. To check the file wasn’t altered in transit, run shasum -a 256 Clearwise-Cleaner-1.2.1.dmg and compare it with the .sha256 file from the release.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no trial, no size limits. The code is open under the MIT license.

Will Clearwise speed up my Mac?

We don’t promise that. The app frees disk space and shows what uses it. When a disk is nearly full macOS does slow down, and cleanup helps. Otherwise speed won’t change.

Could I delete something important by accident?

Only junk that apps recreate on their own is selected for you. Personal files, old documents and duplicates are yours to tick. Everything goes to the Trash, where you can put it back. System folders, Keychain, Mail, Photos and iCloud Drive are protected.

Why does it need Full Disk Access?

macOS hides some cache and log folders even from your own apps. Without access Clearwise still works but finds less. You turn it on in System Settings and the app shows you where. Nothing is sent anywhere: the app doesn’t use the internet.

How is it different from CleanMyMac?

Clearwise is free, open source and has no telemetry, and everything it removes lands in the Trash first. It has no antivirus, app updater or “speed boost”: it focuses on disk space, startup items and a Mac health check.

Which macOS and Macs are supported?

macOS 15 Sequoia or later. One DMG runs on both Apple silicon and Intel.

How do I update or remove Clearwise?

To update, download the new DMG and drag the app into Applications over the old one. To remove it, move it to the Trash.
